Mystery cards

Open one mystery card with your partner. Read the story, talk about it, and decide together what the person should do.

Your tasks

  1. Understand: Identify the person's previous habits, current circumstances, and main difficulty.
  2. Discuss: Suggest two realistic solutions.
  3. Decide: Agree on the best solution and justify your decision.
  4. Prepare: Organize a short oral explanation to present to your classmates.
