💬 Speaking Supporttap a phrase to add it to your notes
1Talking about past habitsused to / would + verb

Use used to for past habits or situations that are no longer true. Use would only for repeated past actions.

2Saying what feels normalbe used to + noun / -ing

Use be used to when something is familiar and no longer strange or difficult.

3Talking about adaptingget used to + noun / -ing

Use get used to for the process of adapting, little by little.

4Suggesting solutionscould · might · would

Use could, might and would to make polite suggestions.

5Agreeing and disagreeingwith your partner

Share your opinion and react politely to your partner's ideas.

6Deciding and presentingbecause · although · the main reason

Connect your decision with clear reasons to convince your classmates.

Unexpected Complication

Challenge:

Challenge their solution

  1. Reconsider: Does the other pair's solution still work with this new problem? Why or why not?
  2. Negotiate: Discuss an alternative with the other pair and agree on a new plan together.
  3. Justify: Explain your new recommendation to your classmates.
